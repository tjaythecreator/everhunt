Privacy policy

Effective date: 15 November 2026

WyrmHunt is made by GameWorld Studios Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales. In this policy "we" means that company.

Questions about your data, or a request to see, correct or delete it: gameworldstudioscustomer@outlook.com.

The short version

  • The game makes an account for you the first time you play. You do not need to give us your name or email.
  • We keep your progress, purchases and clan activity on our servers so your game is safe and the same on every device.
  • Rewarded videos come from Unity LevelPlay. On iPhone and iPad we ask before ads may track you, and you can say no.
  • There is no chat. Other players can see your hero names, your clan's name and motto, and your place on leaderboards.
  • We do not sell your data.
  • You can delete your account at any time from Settings in the game.

1. What we collect and why

WhatWhy

We do not collect your location from GPS, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or health data. We do not ask for your real name, age or birthday.

The game can show reminders on your phone (for example when an egg is ready). These are set on your phone by the game; no data is sent for them. You can turn them off in your phone's settings.

2. Ads

You can watch optional videos for in-game rewards. These come from Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) and Unity Ads, which are run by Unity Technologies. We give LevelPlay your account id so a finished video can be checked before you get your reward.

Ad companies may collect information from your device, such as your device's ad identifier, IP address (from which they can guess your rough area), device type and how you interact with ads.

  • iPhone and iPad: when the game starts, Apple's own pop-up asks if the game may track you. If you say no, ad companies do not get your device's ad identifier. You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • UK, EU, EEA and Switzerland: the game also asks whether ad partners may use your data to pick ads for you. If you say no, you still get videos and the same rewards, just not personalised. You can change this in the game: Settings > Ad Privacy.

Unity's privacy policy: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

3. Who else handles your data

We only share data with companies that help us run the game:

  • Microsoft PlayFab (accounts, saves, gems, purchases, gameplay events) and Microsoft Azure (our game servers and their logs). Privacy: https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/privacy/privacystatement
  • Unity Technologies (game engine, in-app purchase software, LevelPlay and Unity Ads), see section 2.
  • Apple and Google take payments for purchases in the game.
  • Microsoft (Outlook) holds emails you send to support.

We may also share data if the law requires it, to stop fraud, or if the game is sold to another company (they would have to follow this policy).

What other players see: your hero names, your clan's name, tag and motto, your clan role and contributions, and your name and scores on leaderboards. Please do not put personal details in names or mottos.

We do not sell your data.

4. Where your data is kept and for how long

Our service providers may store data in the United States and other countries. Our game servers run in Microsoft Azure's UK West region.

  • Account, save and clan data: while your account exists. Deleted when you delete your account.
  • Server logs and error reports: kept for up to 90 days, then deleted.
  • Purchase records: kept as long as tax and accounting law requires, even after you delete your account.
  • Support emails: up to 2 years after your question is answered.

5. Deleting your account

In the game: Settings > Delete Account > DELETE, then tap again to confirm. You need to be online. This deletes your account, every hero and item, your gems, your save and your clan membership straight away. It cannot be undone, and purchases are not refunded.

Without the game: email gameworldstudioscustomer@outlook.com with the subject "Delete my WyrmHunt account", your hero names and roughly when you last played. We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email. We will never ask for your password.

Deleting the app from your phone does not delete your account.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, to delete it, or to stop using it for some purposes. Email gameworldstudioscustomer@outlook.com. We may ask for details that prove the account is yours (for example your hero names). We answer within one month.

If you are in the UK or EU and are unhappy with our answer, you can complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office: https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/).

7. Children

WyrmHunt is not made for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you think a child under 13 has an account, email us and we will delete it.

8. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top and, for big changes, tell you in the game.
