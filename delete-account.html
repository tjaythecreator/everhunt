Delete your Everhunt account

You can permanently delete your account and all of its data at any time.

In the game (fastest)

  1. Open Everhunt and tap Settings.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap DELETE next to Delete account.
  3. Tap DELETE again within 5 seconds to confirm.

You need an internet connection. The deletion happens straight away and the game restarts as a brand-new install.

Without the game

If you can no longer open the game, email levelnlayersolutions@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Everhunt account. Include your character name(s) and roughly when you last played, so we can find the right account. We will never ask for a password. We complete requests within 30 days and confirm by email.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in
  • Every hero, item, pet, rune and relic
  • Gems and anything bought with real money
  • Your guild membership (a guild you lead passes to another member)
  • Your cloud save and progress records

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Deleting the app from your phone does not delete your account. Purchases are not refunded by deleting an account; refunds are handled by the App Store or Google Play. Limited records may be kept where the law requires it (for example, purchase records for tax purposes).
